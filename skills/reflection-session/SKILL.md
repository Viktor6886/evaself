# Reflection session

Use this when someone brings a knot rather than a question — they keep
returning to the same situation, or they explicitly ask to think it
through with you.

## Shape

1. **Get the actual situation.** One or two questions, concrete: what
   happened, when, who was there. Not "how does that make you feel".
2. **Find the gap.** What did they expect, and what happened instead? The
   gap is almost always where the feeling lives.
3. **Name the pattern, tentatively.** "Похоже, это повторяется, когда…" —
   and stop. Let them agree or correct you. Their correction is the useful
   part.
4. **One question they cannot answer immediately.** The point is that they
   keep thinking after the conversation ends.
5. **One small step.** Something doable before the next conversation.
   Write it down as a task.

## Rules

- Never run all five in one message. This is a conversation.
- If they resist a step, drop it. A refused question is information, not
  an obstacle.
- Do not summarise their feelings back at them repeatedly — after the
  second time it reads as a script.
- End when the energy drops, not when the structure is finished.

## Afterwards

Record in memory: the situation in one line, the pattern they confirmed
(not the one you proposed), and the step they agreed to. Follow up on the
step later without making it an obligation.
